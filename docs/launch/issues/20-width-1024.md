---
title: "Check every screen at 1024 px wide and report any overflow"
labels: good first issue, playtest, ui
---

**What.** `docs/design/EXPERIENCE.md` sets a hard rule: the game works at 1280x800 and up, and nothing overflows at 1024 wide. The screen handoffs say the stacked layout was designed but not looked at: "I did not screenshot 1024" (`docs/handoffs/F-19.md`) and "designed ... but not screenshotted" (`docs/handoffs/F-18.md`).

**What to do.** Start the game, resize the browser window to 1024 px wide (the developer tools' responsive mode does it), and open each screen: the welcome flow, the season map, a level, the terminal, the editor, Logs, Metrics, "What you built", the show-me gallery and the command palette (Cmd+K). Look for horizontal scrolling, clipped buttons, overlapping panes and text that runs out of its box.

**Done when** you have one issue per screen that breaks the rule, each with a screenshot at 1024 px, or one comment here listing the screens you checked and that they were fine.
