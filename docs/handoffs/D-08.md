## What the screen should look like

Page `/arcade`: the usual game header, then a 1200 px column. Small caps "Arcade", the title "THE ARCADE", one sentence.
A grid (1 column below 768 px, 2 up to 1280, 3 at 1280 and above) of cards. The Be the Redis card has an amber edge
(focal): at the top a dark strip with five grey bars and one wide red bar (a slow command in a line, decorative), then
the title in condensed capitals, the pitch, "WHAT IT TEACHES" and the lesson, and at the bottom "BEST SCORE" over a big
number with a primary "Play" button. The coming card is a sunken well: a "COMING" pill, a dimmed title, the pitch, the
sentence with `docs/arcade.md`, a ghost "Copy the path" button.

Cabinet, **before the round** (1280 wide): above, a quiet "All cabinets" button. Header: "ARCADE", "BE THE REDIS", the pitch
on the left; on the right four scorelines (Score in green flip digits, Gave up, Time left `1:00` in amber, Best) and the
buttons Pause (disabled) and Restart (disabled). Below, two columns: left the game area (at least 480 px tall), right a
320 px aside with "WHAT THIS TEACHES" (the lesson, then "Where each rule comes from" collapsed) and "KEYS" (key caps and
words). Under the game area, small print that says it is a simulation. Over the game area a dark veil with one amber-edged
card "YOU ARE REDIS": three numbered lines, a small print line, and the primary button "Start the round" with `Space`. Behind
the veil: "The line" strip (empty: "No one is waiting..."), a "Redis" panel ("Idle. Press Space...") and a "Next command" card.

**During the round**: the veil is gone. "The line": a row of up to six cards, 158 px wide, front first; the first has a
"Next" pill and a glow. Each card: "CLIENT 7", the command in mono (`GET`), its arguments, the key size (`612 bytes`) and a
thin bar that shrinks (green, then amber under half, then red under a quarter; the border follows). More than six: a dashed
"+N waiting" tile. A new client slides in from the right; when the front card is run the others slide left (reduced motion:
they jump). Left below: the "Redis" panel: "Running one command" with the command and a progress bar "n of m ticks"
(red for a slow command, green otherwise) and "Nothing else runs until this one finishes. N waiting." Right below: the
focal "Next command" card: a "Quick" (green) or "Slow" (red) pill, the command large in mono, the server's summary, the
server's "Complexity ..." text, "Key size" and "Holds Redis (simulated)" (e.g. `30 ticks (3.0 s)`), an amber flash line
(for example "Redis is busy: it runs one command at a time." after a press during a command), the buttons "Run it" with
`Space` and, when the command has a cousin, "Swap for SCAN" with `S`. A slow command with no cousin says so in small print.
The third client always brings KEYS, SMEMBERS or HGETALL (a swappable slow command) so the lesson shows in about two
seconds. A swapped client shows "call 2 of 6" and goes to the back of the line after each call.

**Paused**: veil with "PAUSED", "The line waits too.", buttons Resume (Space) and Restart (R).

**After the round**: the veil with an amber card "ROUND OVER": the score in large amber digits and "clients served in
time", a violet "New best" pill when it beat the saved score, "Best so far: n", two sentences of what happened (for
example "You served 60 of 80 clients in time and 20 gave up waiting. 9 of them left while KEYS held Redis for up to 4.2 s
of game time: nothing else ran until it finished."), a boxed "The idea" (one slow command delays every client behind it;
done in small steps the same work lets the others in between), the seed with how to replay it, and "Play again" (Enter).
Space does nothing here on purpose, so a player still tapping does not restart by accident.

## Decisions and surprises
- The digest has no sentence that says "Redis runs every command one at a time". Rule R1 is an inference from four
  lines that each say it for one case (scripts, KEYS, EXEC, the thread note). The cabinet says so in its rule list.
- No `UNLINK` pair: the digest names no DEL/UNLINK rule, so I did not invent one. The swap pairs are KEYS to SCAN
  (digest), SMEMBERS to SSCAN (digest), HGETALL to HSCAN (the pairing is the game's, labelled so).
- The fallback deck is nine commands, because those are the only ones whose real replies exist in the repo
  (`ui/test/terminal.fixtures.ts`); `arcadeBeTheRedis.test.ts` compares the copy with the fixtures through F-22's
  parser. Only KEYS has a cousin offline (SCAN). With the stack up there are 13 commands and three cousins.
- Server deck rule: used only when the server has answered for every command and cousin in the deck (a command it does
  not know is left out; at least six cards, at least one slow one), else the fallback. A half-loaded answer never makes
  a half-live deck. The deck is fixed when a round starts.
- Docs are read through F-22's `docsStore()` (one session cache, no command sent to Redis, nothing sent unless
  `/health` says `commandDocs: true`). I did not write a second reader and did not touch `ui/src/terminal`.
- Tuning (scratch autoplayer under Node, 40 seeds, fallback deck): a player who never swaps loses about 12 of 88 clients
  (13.6 with a slow hand); one who always swaps loses about 0.4 (4 with a slow hand). With a live-like deck the no-swap
  player loses 24 to 28 of 88 and the swapper 4 to 15. These are design checks of the game's own model, not Redis facts,
  and the live-like deck used invented complexity texts (see Assumptions). Patience is 4 s of game time; arrivals every
  9 down to 5 ticks.
- The keyboard: a window `keydown` listener while the cabinet is mounted. A focused button or link keeps Space and Enter
  for itself so nothing fires twice; the Run and Swap buttons do not take focus on mouse down, so Space keeps meaning
  "run" after a click. Holding a key does not repeat.
- `Activity` keeps visited routes mounted and hidden (F-14): the listener and the loop stop with the effects; a hidden
  tab pauses a round.
- Lint is plain recommended rules; there is no `react-hooks` plugin, so I left no disable comments.
- No dependency added, no file outside the spec's list touched (`ui/src/features/arcade/**`, `ui/src/app/arcade/**`,
  `docs/arcade.md`, tests, `scripts/check/D-08.sh`, this file).

## The rules of Be the Redis, and where each comes from

Digest line numbers are lines of `docs/redis-docs-digest.md` on this branch (`ui/test/arcadeFacts.test.ts` checks each
one against the file). "Server docs" means the text of `COMMAND DOCS` as the engine serves it
(`GET /api/command-docs`, docs/handoffs/E-06.md).

| # | Rule the cabinet shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Redis runs one command at a time; while it runs, the other clients wait. | Inferred from digest L300 ("script's execution blocks all server activities during its entire time"), L260 ("KEYS blocks server"), L270 (EXEC: "no other client's request served in the middle"), L42 (threads limited to deleting objects in the background and module blocking commands). No digest line states it for every command: not verified beyond those four. |
| R2 | A slow command delays every client behind it. | Digest L300 ("slow scripts block all clients") and L260. |
| R3 | The summary and the complexity on a card are the server's own words. | Server docs. Offline, a built-in copy of nine replies captured from redis:8 by F-22 (`ui/test/terminal.fixtures.ts`): get, set, hset, zadd, xadd, expire, keys, lpos, scan. |
| R4 | Which commands walk the whole key: HGETALL, SMEMBERS, LRANGE (all), KEYS, LPOS. | HGETALL O(N): digest L108 (and L21). SMEMBERS O(N): L98. LRANGE O(S+N): L87. KEYS: L260 and the server docs. LPOS: the server docs (O(N)) only. |
| R5 | Ticks: O(1) = 1 tick, O(log N) = ceil(log2(N+1)/6) capped at 4, O(N) = ceil(N/100) capped at 60. One tick = 0.1 s of game time. | A simplification. The shape (a walk costs more the bigger the key) is the digest's (L21, L98, L108); the numbers are the game's. The digest gives no real durations. The cabinet says "simulation, not a measurement". |
| R6 | Only the first bound in a complexity text counts ("O(1) for each field/value pair added..." is O(1)). | A simplification. |
| R7 | KEYS can be swapped for SCAN, SMEMBERS for SSCAN, HGETALL for HSCAN. | KEYS/SCAN: digest L260 ("SCAN: incremental, safe for production"). SMEMBERS/SSCAN: L98 ("use `SSCAN`"). HGETALL/HSCAN: L103 lists HSCAN among the hash commands but does not say it replaces HGETALL: the pairing is the game's. |
| R8 | One cursor call costs 1 tick and covers 500 items. | 1 tick: server docs for SCAN ("O(1) for every call"). 500 items: a simplification (L22 shows `COUNT 100` as an example; the default is not in the digest, not verified). |
| R9 | After each cursor call the client's next call joins the back of the line, and the client is patient again. | "Until you reach the cursor value 0" is digest L22. Joining the back and the fresh patience are simplifications. |
| R10 | Commands run in the order they arrived; the player starts the one at the front. | A simplification. |
| R11 | A client gives up after 4 seconds of game time in the line. | A simplification: the game's rule, not a Redis fact. |
| R12 | Score = clients served in time. A round is one minute of game time. | A simplification: the game's scoring. |

(R1 to R12 are `FACTS` in `cabinets/be-the-redis/facts.ts`, in this order.)
