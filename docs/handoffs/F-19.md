# F-19 handoff: the season map and the caps shelf

## What I built
The home screen (`/`) is now the league season on a night campus. Top to bottom:

1. **Greeting and scoreboard strip** (`map/Scoreboard.tsx`): "Hello, <name>" (h1, kept), total XP (`xp` test id,
   plain number so `toHaveText('85')` still works), rank from `rankFor` with a bar and "N XP to <next rank>", levels
   passed out of total, match nights complete out of 8.
2. **Next match** (`map/NextMatchCard.tsx`, the one loud thing: focal `Card`, floodlight beams, a hollow match
   number behind the copy): chapter eyebrow, the incident's first message as the headline with sender and time,
   level number and title, mode and XP pills, one primary action, "Play" or "Continue" (a real link styled as the
   primary button, `next-match-play`), and a "This over" box with the current over's balls, big. First
   `in_progress` level, else first `available` (`nextMatch` in `map/model.ts`). When nothing is left:
   **Season complete** card (`season-complete`) with a link to What you built.
3. **Caps shelf** (`map/CapsShelf.tsx`, `map/CapEmblem.tsx`, `map/emblem.ts`), beside the hero: badges as enamel
   pins, newest first, on lit ledges; name in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus and as the accessible name;
   count (`caps-count`); the newest seven plus a "+N" chip; "All caps" opens a `Dialog` with every cap and its name.
   Emblems are generated from the badge name only (FNV-1a hash): one of 6 shapes (round, shield, hex, rosette,
   octagon, crest), one of 7 palette enamels, gold or silver rim (amber enamel always silver), one of 4 motifs
   (plain, band, stars, laurel), one or two stamped initials ("Fresh on write" -> FW, small words skipped). Caps
   pop in with the `pop` variant and catch a shine on hover. Empty shelf: a dashed "?" slot and "Your first cap
   is waiting."
4. **The season** (`map/SeasonPath.tsx`, `map/CampusScene.tsx`): the 8 chapters as fixture cards in a serpentine
   (4 columns; 2 below 860px of map width; row 2 runs right to left) over an SVG campus (sky, twinkling stars,
   hostel skyline with lit windows, the ground oval, two floodlight pylons whose four beams switch on as chapters
   complete). An SVG road, measured from the cards with `ResizeObserver` (offsets, so hover lifts never bend it),
   arches between fixture nodes and bends round the end of a row: fully lit amber after a complete chapter,
   partly lit out of the current chapter by its passed fraction, a faint dashed track (drifting forward) beyond.
   Each fixture: node disc (complete = lit amber with a turf check; current = amber ring, pulse and a "Next up"
   tag; upcoming = dim with a lock), "Match 0N", state word, title, incident headline (2 lines), the levels as
   balls in overs of six (`map/BallStrip.tsx`), "N of M" and a bar. Legend of ball meanings above the map.
   - Balls use `ballFor` (symbol and tone are the face; `label` is the tooltip and accessible name:
     "Level 3, Say hello: Passed first time"). The current ball pulses. Unlocked balls are links to the level
     (`prefetch={false}`); locked balls are `role="img"`. Each strip is one tab stop with arrow keys / Home / End
     (`map/roving.ts`), so the page does not get 96 tab stops.
5. **Fixture detail** (`map/FixtureDetail.tsx`): clicking a fixture (its title button covers the card,
   `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="fixture-detail"`) opens it under the map (scrolls into view if needed, plays
   `tick`). Defaults to the chapter of the next match; resets to it when you come back to the route (Activity
   keeps the route mounted, see below). Header: match, title, headline, progress bar, "Read the chapter thread"
   (opens a `Dialog` with `Thread({ messages, label })`, unchanged props). Rows in two columns: number, ball,
   title, mode with icon, XP, status pill (Passed / Ready / In progress / Locked, the old words), link to
   `/level/<id>` when unlocked, `aria-disabled` span when locked.
6. **Loading and error**: while chapters are null, skeletons in the final layout (plus the old "Loading the map..."
   text for screen readers). The store swallows a failed `GET /api/chapters`; the screen calls
   `refreshChapters()` once itself and, if chapters are still null, shows "Rain delay on the fixture list"
   (`map-error`, `role="alert"`) with "Try again" (retries through the store and recovers without a reload).

Files: `ui/src/screens/ChapterMap.tsx`, `ui/src/map/{model.ts,emblem.ts,roving.ts,BallStrip.tsx,CampusScene.tsx,
CapEmblem.tsx,CapsShelf.tsx,FixtureDetail.tsx,NextMatchCard.tsx,Scoreboard.tsx,SeasonPath.tsx,map.module.css}`,
`ui/test/map.test.tsx`, `scripts/check/F-19.sh`. `ui/src/app/page.tsx` unchanged (already a static shell with
`instant = false`). No dependency added; icons from lucide-react; SVG and CSS only.

## Surprises
- Zustand v5 static rendering always uses the initial state (`getInitialState` is captured inside the vanilla
  store; assigning `useQuest.getInitialState` does nothing), hence the `vi.mock` in the test.
- A card that lifts on hover (transform) becomes a stacking context, so its ball tooltip fell under the next card's
  balls; the hovered or focused card now gets `z-20`.
- The legacy `.badges` rule is unlayered (flex row, margin), so the class sits on a wrapper div, not the card.
