# Code of conduct

Redis Quest is a place where people learn. Anyone who opens an issue, sends a pull request, reviews one or
writes in a discussion is expected to keep it that way.

## What we ask

- Be kind and be specific. Say what you saw and what you expected, and assume the other person is trying to help.
- Criticise the work, not the person. "This level never says what the key is called" is useful. A comment
  about someone's skill is not.
- Beginners are the audience of the game and are welcome in the project. A question that shows a level was
  confusing is a bug report, not a mistake.
- Credit other people's work. Do not paste in text, code or images you do not have the right to share.
- Respect people's time. Say when you cannot finish something you started.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, slurs, harassment, threats, or sexual language or images.
- Personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information.
- Repeatedly pushing a request after the maintainer has said no.
- Spam, advertising, or unrelated promotion.

## What happens

The maintainer can edit or delete comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people from the
repository when this page is not followed. A first, small problem usually gets a short reply asking for a change.

To report something that happened here, open an issue that does not repeat the harmful content, or follow the
contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if you would rather not post publicly. Reports are read by the
maintainer and are not shared beyond what is needed to deal with them.

This page applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any space opened in its name.
